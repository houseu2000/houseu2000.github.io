Research

Research at the intersection of vision-based structural sensing and bridge monitoring data.

01

Vision-Based Structural Sensing and Post-Disaster Assessment

Using urban surveillance video and computer vision to recover structural motion and inter-storey displacement from noisy, occluded footage, enabling scalable post-disaster assessment.

  • Inter-storey displacement
  • Occlusion-robust tracking
  • Camera motion removal
  • Non-contact response reconstruction
02

Data-Driven Bridge Structural Health Monitoring and Condition Assessment

Developing data-driven methods for long-span bridge monitoring, including vortex-induced vibration recognition, anomaly cleaning, missing-data imputation, and structural damage identification.

  • VIV recognition
  • Monitoring data cleaning
  • Missing-data imputation
  • Damage identification
